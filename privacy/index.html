Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Screendial is a desktop assistant, made by Gropad Inc ("Gropad", "we", "us"), that looks at your screen when you ask it to and helps you understand what you see. This policy explains what information Screendial handles, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a securely stored password, used to sign you in.
  • Screenshots. When you ask Screendial a question, it takes a screenshot of each of your displays and sends it, with your question, to be analyzed. Screenshots are taken only when you send a question, not continuously.
  • Voice. When you use the microphone or say "Screendial", your recording is sent to be transcribed. When speaker output is on, the response text is sent to be turned into speech.
  • Wake word. While Screendial is open, it listens for the word "Screendial" on your device. This listening happens locally and is not recorded or sent anywhere until the wake word is detected.
  • Conversation history. Your recent questions and Screendial's answers are kept on your device so follow-up questions have context. Clearing the conversation in the app, or restarting it, starts a fresh conversation.
  • Request logs. For each question, we keep a log of the request and response: your question as text, the name of the app you were using, the names and sizes of your displays, what Screendial's AI models were given and answered at each step (including the actions it chose and their results), any errors, and timings. Logs never contain your screenshots or voice recordings, only their file sizes.
  • Plan and usage. Your plan, how many questions you've asked each month (to apply your plan's monthly limit), and, if you subscribe, your subscription status and renewal date. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • App context. The name of the app you're using, so Screendial can tailor its guidance.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide Screendial: to sign you in, to apply your plan and bill your subscription, to answer your questions about your screen, to transcribe and speak responses, and to keep your conversation history. We use request logs to debug problems and improve Screendial. A small number of authorized Gropad staff can review these logs for those purposes. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

Screendial relies on a small number of providers to process your requests:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, request log storage, and the secure servers that relay your requests.
  • Google (Gemini): analyzes your screenshots and questions to produce answers.
  • ElevenLabs: transcribes your voice and generates spoken responses.
  • Stripe: processes subscription payments and stores your payment details.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Where your data is stored

Your account and request logs are stored with Supabase in the United States (US East). Your requests are also processed by Google and ElevenLabs, which may handle them in other locations under their own terms.

Training

Gropad does not use your screenshots, voice, or conversations to train AI models.

Permissions

Screendial asks your operating system for screen recording and microphone access. You can revoke either at any time in your system settings; the related features will stop working until access is restored.

Retention and deletion

Screenshots and voice recordings are used to answer your request and are not stored by Screendial afterwards. Clearing the conversation in the app starts a fresh conversation; past conversations are kept in our request logs until you delete your account, and are then deleted with it. Monthly question counts and subscription records are kept until you delete your account; Stripe keeps payment records as required for tax and accounting. Deleting your account cancels any active subscription. You can export your history and delete your account and its data at any time from your account page, or by contacting us at support@gropad.com.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to stored data is restricted to your account and to the authorized Gropad staff described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Screendial is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app or on this site.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@gropad.com.
