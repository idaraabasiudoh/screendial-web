Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Screendial is a desktop assistant, made by Gropad Inc ("Gropad", "we", "us"), that looks at your screen when you ask it to and helps you understand what you see. This policy explains what information Screendial handles, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a securely stored password, used to sign you in.
  • Screenshots. When you ask Screendial a question, it takes a screenshot of each of your displays and sends it, with your question, to be analyzed. Screenshots are taken only when you send a question, not continuously.
  • Voice. When you use the microphone or say "Screendial", your recording is sent to be transcribed. When speaker output is on, the response text is sent to be turned into speech.
  • Wake word. While Screendial is open, it listens for the word "Screendial" on your device. This listening happens locally and is not recorded or sent anywhere until the wake word is detected.
  • Conversation history. Your typed questions and Screendial's answers are saved to your account so the conversation can continue. You can clear the conversation from the app at any time.
  • App context. The name of the app you're using, so Screendial can tailor its guidance.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide Screendial: to sign you in, to answer your questions about your screen, to transcribe and speak responses, and to keep your conversation history. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

Screendial relies on a small number of providers to process your requests:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, conversation storage, and the secure servers that relay your requests.
  • Google (Gemini): analyzes your screenshots and questions to produce answers.
  • ElevenLabs: transcribes your voice and generates spoken responses.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Permissions

Screendial asks your operating system for screen recording and microphone access. You can revoke either at any time in your system settings; the related features will stop working until access is restored.

Retention and deletion

Screenshots and voice recordings are used to answer your request and are not stored by Screendial afterwards. Conversation history is kept until you clear it or delete your account. You can view your history and delete your account and its data at any time from your account page, or by contacting us at support@gropad.com.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to stored data is restricted to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Screendial is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app or on this site.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@gropad.com.
